Water storage calculator

Ready.gov says to store at least one gallon of water per person per day, for several days, for drinking and sanitation. In very hot weather, needs can double. This tool does that math for you.

Ready.gov does not set a single official amount for pets. The pet gallons field is a planning number you can change. It is not an official requirement.

Source: Ready.gov, Water. A gallon of water weighs about 8.3 pounds. Ready.gov also notes that children, nursing mothers, and people who are sick may need more than the baseline.

If you fill your own food-grade containers, Ready.gov says to replace that water about every six months. Sealed store-bought water can stay in a cool, dark place in its original container.
