Food storage shelf-life lookup

Search a food name and see the official USDA FoodKeeper storage times. These figures are about quality and storage, not a personal food-safety ruling. When in doubt, use the official FoodKeeper app or site.

    Source: USDA FSIS FoodKeeper. General storage habits also appear on Ready.gov, Food.

    FoodKeeper times often start from the date of purchase. After opening, use the "after opening" line when one is listed.
