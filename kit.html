72-hour kit builder

Ready.gov says a basic kit should cover several days of water, food, and other supplies, and that you should keep one at home, and consider separate kits for work and the car. This builder sizes a 3-day list to the people in your house.

This is a packing guide, not a free product. If you want a printable household checklist, Prepper Daily's 72-Hour Kit Checklist is a $5 paid download. You do not need it to use this list.

Sources: Ready.gov, Build a kit and Ready.gov, Water. The car list also follows Ready.gov winter weather guidance.
